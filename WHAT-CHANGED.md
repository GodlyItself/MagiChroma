## ADDED `import` statement
you can do `from name import name2` or `import name2`
Here, `name2` can be `everything`, content names seperated by comma like `func1, const2, var3` or simply `stuff`
On the other hand `name` is merely the name of the file.

You can also use aliases like `...import name as stuff`
Here, `stuff` will be the new name of `name`. Again, here no commas or `everything` can be used. That's why I used `name` isntead of `name2`.
